# Phase 0 — Discovery Brief

## DJ Tenkay Brand Brief

**Completed:** Sprint 0 (Pre-development)

## Identity

**DJ Name:** DJ Tenkay

**Hook Line:**
> "You don't want 100, you want Tenkay."

**Tagline:**
> "Open-format. Every genre. Every room. One name."

**Specialty Genres:** Afrobeats · Amapiano · Trap
**Also Explores:** R&B · Pop · House

---

## Niche

**Major Events:**
- ASA Weekend
- College weekend events
- Weddings

**Private Events:**
- Birthday parties
- Pool parties
- Cookouts
- Graduation parties

---

## Target Audience (Who Books)

- Event planners
- Campus organizations and promoters
- Friends and personal network

---

## Business Goals

- Get booked for 3 big events
- Grow TikTok and Instagram following by 20+ through gigs and DJ content

---

## Brand Feel

- Underground rapper aesthetic (reference: Pz', Øway)
- Dark, raw, textured — with a welcoming energy underneath
- Visual references: Spotify dark UI, Apple Music dark mode
- Confident, minimal, music-first

## Summary

DJ Tenkay's brand sits at the intersection of underground music culture and welcoming energy. The website should feel like opening Spotify in a dark room — immersive, music-forward, and immediately communicating "this person moves crowds." The hook line does the heavy lifting; everything else supports it quietly.
